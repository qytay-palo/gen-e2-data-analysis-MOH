# E06-S07: Develop Business Cases for Capacity Expansion

**Story ID**: E06-S07  
**Epic**: EPIC-006 - Predictive Demand Forecasting for Capacity Planning

## Parent Epic
Predictive Demand Forecasting for Capacity Planning - Building validated demand forecasting models to predict patient visits and provide evidence-based capacity recommendations for strategic planning.

## Overview and Statement

Create detailed business cases for minimum 3 priority capacity expansion opportunities with ROI analysis, implementation plans, and decision support.

**As a** healthcare investment decision maker  
**I want to** receive business cases for capacity expansion investments  
**So that** I can evaluate options and make informed capital allocation decisions

### Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Select minimum 3 priority capacity expansion opportunities based on gap analysis
- [ ] Each business case includes: demand projection, capacity gap, proposed solution, capital cost, operating cost, benefits, ROI, implementation timeline
- [ ] Calculate ROI and payback period for each option
- [ ] Provide scenario analysis (optimistic, base, pessimistic)
- [ ] Compare alternative solutions (expansion vs. new build vs. redistribution)
- [ ] Assess implementation risks and mitigation strategies
- [ ] Create executive summaries suitable for investment committees
- [ ] Generate detailed financial models with multi-year projections

### Technical Notes
- Use standard capital budgeting techniques (NPV, IRR, payback)
- Discount future cash flows appropriately
- Monetize benefits: increased capacity, reduced wait times, improved outcomes
- Include sensitivity analysis on key assumptions
- Reference construction and operational cost benchmarks
- Consider phased implementation options
- Address non-financial factors (strategic fit, equity, risk)

7 days
