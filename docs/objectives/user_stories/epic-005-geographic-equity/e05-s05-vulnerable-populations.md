# E05-S05: Profile Vulnerable Populations at Risk

**Story ID**: E05-S05  
**Epic**: EPIC-005 - Geographic Access & Health Equity Analysis

## Parent Epic
Geographic Access & Health Equity Analysis - Conducting geographic access analysis and equity assessment to identify underserved areas requiring intervention and promote health equity across Singapore.

## Overview and Statement

Identify and profile vulnerable populations (elderly, disabled, low-income) in underserved areas to understand who is most affected by access barriers and their specific needs.

**As a** public health planner  
**I want to** understand which vulnerable populations have poor healthcare access  
**So that** I can design targeted interventions addressing their specific needs

### Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Identify vulnerable population segments: elderly (65+), disabled, chronic disease patients, low-income
- [ ] Quantify vulnerable population counts in underserved areas
- [ ] Profile health needs and service requirements of vulnerable groups
- [ ] Map concentration of vulnerable populations across geography
- [ ] Assess access barriers specific to vulnerable groups (mobility, affordability, awareness)
- [ ] Create population-at-risk profiles by underserved area
- [ ] Estimate health impact of access barriers on vulnerable populations
- [ ] Generate vulnerable population report with intervention recommendations

### Technical Notes
- Use age distribution data to identify elderly concentrations
- May need to infer vulnerability from available demographic and health data
- Consider intersection of multiple vulnerability factors
- Link vulnerable populations to health outcome data where available
- Prioritize interventions by vulnerable population density
- Consider different intervention needs for different vulnerable groups
