# E03-S05: Identify Policy and Governance Gaps

**Story ID**: E03-S05  
**Epic**: EPIC-003 - Healthcare System Gap Analysis & Policy Recommendations

## Parent Epic
Healthcare System Gap Analysis & Policy Recommendations - Conducting comprehensive gap analysis across the care continuum to identify high-impact intervention opportunities enabling evidence-based policy decisions.

## Overview and Statement

Examine existing healthcare policies, regulations, and governance structures to identify gaps requiring new policies, regulatory changes, or structural reforms.

**As a** policy maker  
**I want to** identify specific policy and governance gaps in the healthcare system  
**So that** I can develop targeted policy interventions and regulatory reforms

### Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Review existing healthcare policies across domains (access, quality, financing, workforce, prevention)
- [ ] Identify minimum 3 policy gaps requiring new regulations or policy changes
- [ ] Document minimum 1 governance structure improvement opportunity
- [ ] Assess policy implementation gaps (policies exist but not enforced)
- [ ] Identify regulatory barriers to innovation or efficiency
- [ ] Document evidence of policy ineffectiveness or unintended consequences
- [ ] Prioritize policy gaps by impact and urgency
- [ ] Create policy gap inventory with reform recommendations

### Technical Notes
- Combine quantitative analysis (outcomes, trends) with qualitative assessment
- Look for patterns suggesting policy gaps (persistent problems despite resources)
- Consider international best practices in policy design
- Identify quick policy wins vs. longer-term structural reforms
- Assess political and implementation feasibility
- Document stakeholder perspectives where available

### Estimated Effort
5 days

### Priority
High

- E03-S03: Benefits from service gap findings
- E03-S04: Benefits from resource gap findings
