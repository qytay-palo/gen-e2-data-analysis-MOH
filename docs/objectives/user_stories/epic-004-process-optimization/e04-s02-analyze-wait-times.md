# E04-S02: Analyze Wait Times and Service Delays

**Story ID**: E04-S02  
**Epic**: EPIC-004 - Process Optimization & Improvement Opportunities

## Parent Epic
Process Optimization & Improvement Opportunities - Conducting patient journey mapping, wait time analysis, and best practice identification to document validated improvement opportunities with quantified business value.

## Overview and Statement

Conduct comprehensive analysis of wait times and service delays across the healthcare system to quantify delay patterns, identify root causes, and highlight reduction opportunities.

**As a** operations manager  
**I want to** understand where and why patients experience long wait times  
**So that** I can target interventions to reduce delays and improve patient flow

### Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Analyze wait time patterns by facility, service type, and time period
- [ ] Calculate average, median, and 95th percentile wait times
- [ ] Identify facilities and services with longest delays
- [ ] Analyze temporal patterns (day of week, season, time of day if data permits)
- [ ] Correlate wait times with capacity utilization and staffing levels
- [ ] Identify root causes of delays (capacity constraints, process inefficiencies, demand spikes)
- [ ] Quantify patient volume affected by excessive wait times
- [ ] Create wait time analysis report with visualizations

### Technical Notes
- Use queuing theory where applicable (arrival rates, service rates)
- Define "excessive" wait time thresholds by service type
- Consider industry benchmarks for acceptable wait times
- Use statistical methods to identify significant patterns
- Analyze relationships between wait times and outcomes
- Focus on actionable insights rather than just descriptive stats
