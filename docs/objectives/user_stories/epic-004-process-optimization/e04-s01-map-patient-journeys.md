# E04-S01: Map Patient Journeys Across Care Pathways

**Story ID**: E04-S01  
**Epic**: EPIC-004 - Process Optimization & Improvement Opportunities

## Parent Epic
Process Optimization & Improvement Opportunities - Conducting patient journey mapping, wait time analysis, and best practice identification to document validated improvement opportunities with quantified business value.

## Overview and Statement

Create detailed patient journey maps for major care pathways (emergency care, elective surgery, outpatient visits, chronic disease management) to visualize the end-to-end patient experience and identify friction points.

**As a** process improvement specialist  
**I want to** map complete patient journeys from entry to exit across different care pathways  
**So that** I can visualize the entire process and identify where delays and inefficiencies occur

### Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Map minimum 4 major patient journeys: emergency care, elective procedures, outpatient consultations, chronic disease management
- [ ] Document all process steps, decision points, and handoffs
- [ ] Identify patient touchpoints and service interactions
- [ ] Estimate time spent at each stage (where data permits)
- [ ] Highlight pain points, delays, and bottlenecks
- [ ] Create visual journey maps using flowcharts or swim lane diagrams
- [ ] Document data sources and assumptions used in mapping
- [ ] Validate maps with stakeholder input where possible

### Technical Notes
- Use healthcare utilization data to understand patient flow patterns
- Consider different patient segments (age, condition, urgency)
- Document variations in journeys across facilities
- Use process mapping notation (BPMN or similar)
- Focus on measurable stages where data exists
- Identify where data gaps prevent complete mapping
