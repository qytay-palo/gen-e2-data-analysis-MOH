# E04-S05: Identify Quality Enhancement Opportunities

**Story ID**: E04-S05  
**Epic**: EPIC-004 - Process Optimization & Improvement Opportunities

## Parent Epic
Process Optimization & Improvement Opportunities - Conducting patient journey mapping, wait time analysis, and best practice identification to document validated improvement opportunities with quantified business value.

## Overview and Statement

Document minimum 4 quality enhancement opportunities that improve care outcomes, reduce errors, or enhance care coordination with measurable quality metrics.

**As a** quality improvement officer  
**I want to** identify specific quality improvements that will enhance patient outcomes  
**So that** I can implement interventions that improve the quality and safety of care

### Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Identify minimum 4 quality enhancement opportunities across care quality, safety, coordination, outcomes
- [ ] Each opportunity includes: quality gap identified, proposed improvement, expected quality metrics improvement, patient impact
- [ ] Link quality issues to process or resource factors where evident in data
- [ ] Quantify patient population affected by quality issues
- [ ] Assess implementation requirements and barriers
- [ ] Define success metrics (quality indicators, outcome measures)
- [ ] Create detailed opportunity briefs with improvement plans
- [ ] Prioritize by patient safety impact and outcome improvement potential

### Technical Notes
- Use mortality, readmission, or complication data if available
- Look for quality variation across facilities
- Consider care coordination gaps and handoff failures
- Reference clinical quality standards and guidelines
- Focus on measurable quality indicators
- Consider patient safety alongside clinical outcomes
- Link to patient satisfaction where data exists

### Estimated Effort
5 days

### Priority
High

## Dependencies

- May benefit from outcome data not fully detailed in available datasets
