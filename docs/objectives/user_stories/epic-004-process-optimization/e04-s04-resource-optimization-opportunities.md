# E04-S04: Identify Resource Optimization Opportunities

**Story ID**: E04-S04  
**Epic**: EPIC-004 - Process Optimization & Improvement Opportunities

## Parent Epic
Process Optimization & Improvement Opportunities - Conducting patient journey mapping, wait time analysis, and best practice identification to document validated improvement opportunities with quantified business value.

## Overview and Statement

Document minimum 3 resource optimization opportunities addressing staffing patterns, equipment utilization, and capacity management with quantified ROI.

**As a** resource planning manager  
**I want to** identify how to better utilize existing resources  
**So that** I can improve efficiency without requiring significant new investments

### Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Identify minimum 3 resource optimization opportunities
- [ ] Analyze staffing patterns against demand patterns (identify over/under-staffing)
- [ ] Assess equipment and facility utilization rates
- [ ] Identify opportunities for resource sharing or reallocation
- [ ] Quantify potential cost savings and efficiency gains
- [ ] Estimate implementation complexity and change management requirements
- [ ] Create business cases with ROI calculations
- [ ] Provide implementation recommendations with timelines

### Technical Notes
- Use utilization data to identify underutilized resources
- Compare staffing patterns to patient arrival patterns
- Consider shift scheduling optimization
- Analyze seasonal and temporal demand variations
- Calculate opportunity costs of idle resources
- Reference workforce optimization best practices
- Consider constraint theory (Theory of Constraints)

5 days

### Priority
High

- E04-S02: Requires wait time and capacity analysis
