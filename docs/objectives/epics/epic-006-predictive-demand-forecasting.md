## Problem Statement

Healthcare administrators lack predictive tools for future demand, making capacity planning reactive rather than proactive, resulting in overcrowding or underutilization. By building validated demand forecasting models, we can predict patient visits 1-year and 5-years ahead, model demographic impacts, and provide evidence-based capacity recommendations, enabling strategic planning and optimized resource allocation.

## Objectives

1. **Demand Forecasting Models**: Develop time-series and machine learning models to forecast patient visits by facility, specialty, and time period
2. **Demographic Impact Modeling**: Model effects of population growth, aging, and demographic shifts on healthcare demand
3. **Capacity Gap Analysis**: Compare forecasted demand against current capacity to identify gaps
4. **Resource Needs Projection**: Predict future resource requirements (staff, equipment, beds, space)
5. **Business Case Development**: Create business cases for facility expansions or new builds with ROI analysis

## Success Criteria

✅ Forecasting models achieve 85%+ accuracy (≤15% MAPE)
✅ 1-year and 5-year demand forecasts by facility
✅ Demographic shift impact analysis (population growth, aging effects)
✅ Capacity gap identification (facilities projected to exceed 90% utilization)
✅ Resource needs projections with confidence intervals
✅ Business cases for minimum 3 capacity expansion opportunities

## Key Deliverables

- Demand forecasting models (ARIMA, Prophet, regression, random forest)
- 1-year and 5-year demand projections by facility
- Demographic impact analysis report
- Capacity gap analysis (demand vs. current capacity)
- Resource needs projections (staff, beds, equipment)
- Business cases for facility expansions with ROI
- Capacity planning dashboard
- Strategic planning recommendations
