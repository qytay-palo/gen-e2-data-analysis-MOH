# Analytics Epics - Strategic Initiatives

## Overview

**Total Epics**: 6  
**Critical Priority**: 3 (EPIC-001, EPIC-002, EPIC-003)  
**High Priority**: 2 (EPIC-004, EPIC-005)  
**Medium Priority**: 1 (EPIC-006)  
**Estimated Total Duration**: 14-18 weeks (with parallel execution: 6-8 weeks)

**Project Period**: 2 February 2026 - 13 April 2026 (10 weeks)

---

## Epic Roadmap (Prioritized)

### Critical Priority (Start Immediately - Weeks 1-5)

#### 1. **[EPIC-001: Healthcare Facility Utilization & Bottleneck Analysis](epic-001-facility-utilization-bottleneck-analysis.md)** ⭐ CRITICAL
**Category**: Descriptive & Diagnostic | **Duration**: 2-3 weeks | **Dependencies**: None

Profile facility utilization patterns and identify minimum 10 operational bottlenecks across Singapore's healthcare network. Enables resource optimization and addresses Success Metric #1 (Bottleneck Identification).

**Key Deliverables**:
- Facility utilization profiles for 100% of healthcare facilities
- Top 10 bottlenecks with severity scores and root cause analysis
- Interactive performance dashboard
- 5+ improvement recommendations with roadmaps

**Priority Score**: 125 (Impact: 5, Feasibility: 5, Urgency: 5)

---

#### 2. **[EPIC-002: Disease Outbreak Detection & Surveillance System](epic-002-disease-outbreak-surveillance-system.md)** ⭐ CRITICAL  
**Category**: Predictive & Diagnostic | **Duration**: 4-5 weeks | **Dependencies**: None

Establish automated disease surveillance system with anomaly detection, spatial clustering, and epidemic forecasting. Addresses Outcome #1 (Disease Outbreak Detection & Surveillance).

**Key Deliverables**:
- Real-time surveillance dashboard for 10+ key diseases
- Anomaly detection system with <5% false positive rate
- Spatial clustering analysis identifying disease hotspots
- Epidemic forecasting models (≤15% MAPE)
- Outbreak alert system and response playbook

**Priority Score**: 100 (Impact: 5, Feasibility: 4, Urgency: 5)

---

#### 3. **[EPIC-003: Healthcare System Gap Analysis & Policy Recommendations](epic-003-healthcare-system-gap-analysis.md)** ⭐ CRITICAL
**Category**: Diagnostic & Prescriptive | **Duration**: 3-4 weeks | **Dependencies**: None

Conduct systematic gap analysis across healthcare continuum to identify minimum 8 high-impact policy intervention opportunities. Addresses Outcome #3 (Policy Need Identification) and Success Metric #2.

**Key Deliverables**:
- Minimum 8 policy intervention opportunities categorized by type
- Evidence-based policy recommendations with impact assessments
- Priority ranking framework
- Cost-benefit analysis for top interventions
- Policy briefs for government stakeholders

**Priority Score**: 100 (Impact: 5, Feasibility: 5, Urgency: 4)

---

### High Priority (Weeks 3-7)

#### 4. **[EPIC-004: Process Optimization & Improvement Opportunities](epic-004-process-optimization-improvement.md)** ⭐ HIGH
**Category**: Diagnostic & Prescriptive | **Duration**: 3-4 weeks | **Dependencies**: EPIC-001 (recommended)

Analyze patient care processes to identify minimum 15 improvement opportunities through patient journey mapping, wait time analysis, and best practice identification. Addresses Outcome #4 (Process Improvement) and Success Metric #3.

**Key Deliverables**:
- Patient journey maps with bottleneck identification
- Wait time analysis and efficiency metrics
- Minimum 15 validated improvement opportunities
- Best practice playbooks from high-performing facilities
- Resource optimization recommendations

**Priority Score**: 80 (Impact: 4, Feasibility: 5, Urgency: 4)

---

#### 5. **[EPIC-005: Geographic Access & Health Equity Analysis](epic-005-geographic-access-equity.md)** ⭐ HIGH
**Category**: Equity & Descriptive | **Duration**: 3-4 weeks | **Dependencies**: None

Quantify geographic healthcare access disparities and identify underserved populations requiring targeted interventions. Addresses Outcome #2 (Facility Utilization Analysis) and equity goals.

**Key Deliverables**:
- Geographic access maps and accessibility scores
- Identification of 3+ underserved geographic areas
- Health equity scorecard with disparity metrics
- Population at-risk quantification
- Recommendations for new facilities or mobile clinics

**Priority Score**: 75 (Impact: 5, Feasibility: 4, Urgency: 4)

---

### Medium Priority (Weeks 6-10)

#### 6. **[EPIC-006: Predictive Demand Forecasting for Capacity Planning](epic-006-predictive-demand-forecasting.md)** 
**Category**: Predictive | **Duration**: 4-5 weeks | **Dependencies**: EPIC-001 (baseline data)

Build demand forecasting models to predict future healthcare utilization and support strategic capacity planning decisions. Addresses Outcome #2 (Facility Utilization Analysis).

**Key Deliverables**:
- 1-year and 5-year demand forecasts by facility
- Demographic shift impact modeling
- Capacity gap analysis and recommendations
- Resource needs projections (staff, equipment, space)
- Business cases for facility expansions

**Priority Score**: 60 (Impact: 4, Feasibility: 4, Urgency: 4)

## Risk Management Summary

### High-Risk Epics (Require Careful Management)

**EPIC-002** (Disease Surveillance):
- Complex spatial/temporal algorithms
- Limited data granularity (quarterly vs. weekly)
- Risk of alert fatigue from false positives

**Mitigation**: Start early, allocate buffer time, iterative development

**EPIC-005** (Geographic Equity):
- May require external geographic data
- Limited spatial resolution in dataset
- Complex accessibility modeling

**Mitigation**: Assess data early, prepare fallback approaches, leverage proxies
