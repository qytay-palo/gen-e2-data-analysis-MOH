## Problem Statement

Not all populations have equal geographic access to healthcare services, creating disparities in health outcomes, but systematic quantification of access gaps is lacking. By conducting geographic access analysis and equity assessment, we can identify minimum 3 underserved areas requiring intervention, quantify affected populations, and provide evidence-based recommendations for new facilities or mobile clinics, promoting health equity across Singapore.

## Objectives

1. **Geographic Access Mapping**: Calculate travel distance/time from residential areas to nearest healthcare facilities
2. **Underserved Area Identification**: Identify minimum 3 geographic areas with limited healthcare access (>5km from nearest facility)
3. **Health Equity Quantification**: Calculate disparity metrics (Gini coefficient, concentration indices) across demographic groups
4. **Population At-Risk**: Quantify affected populations including vulnerable groups (elderly, low-income, disabled)
5. **Intervention Recommendations**: Recommend locations for new facilities, mobile clinics, or telemedicine services

## Success Criteria

✅ Comprehensive geographic access maps for all planning areas
✅ Identify minimum 3 underserved geographic areas requiring intervention
✅ Quantify affected populations with demographic profiles
✅ Health equity scorecard with disparity metrics
✅ Accessibility scores by geography (0-100 scale)
✅ Evidence-based recommendations for facility placement or mobile services

## Key Deliverables

- Geographic access maps and heatmaps
- Healthcare desert identification (areas >5km from facilities)
- Health equity scorecard with disparity quantification
- Population at-risk profiles (number, demographics, health needs)
- Recommendations for new facilities or mobile clinics
- Interactive geographic dashboard
- Policy brief on healthcare access inequities
