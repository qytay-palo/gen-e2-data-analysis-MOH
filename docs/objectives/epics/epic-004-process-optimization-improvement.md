## Problem Statement

Healthcare processes contain inefficiencies and bottlenecks that reduce patient throughput, increase wait times, and frustrate patients, but systematic process analysis is lacking. By conducting patient journey mapping, wait time analysis, and best practice identification, we can document minimum 15 validated improvement opportunities with quantified business value, enabling operational excellence and enhanced patient experience.

## Objectives

1. **Patient Journey Mapping**: Map end-to-end patient journeys identifying friction points and delays
2. **Wait Time Analysis**: Analyze wait time patterns to identify root causes and reduction opportunities
3. **Improvement Identification**: Document minimum 15 improvement opportunities across process efficiency, resource optimization, quality, and patient experience
4. **Best Practice Discovery**: Identify and document best practices from high-performing facilities
5. **ROI Quantification**: Calculate expected improvement and ROI for each opportunity

## Success Criteria

✅ Document minimum 15 validated improvement opportunities:
- 5+ process efficiency improvements (wait time reduction, workflow streamlining)
- 3+ resource optimization opportunities
- 4+ quality enhancement opportunities
- 3+ patient experience improvements

✅ Each opportunity includes:
- Current state baseline metrics
- Expected improvement (quantified outcome)
- Implementation complexity rating (low/medium/high)
- Expected ROI or cost savings
- Implementation roadmap

✅ Patient journey maps for major care pathways
✅ Best practice playbooks from high-performing facilities
✅ Resource optimization recommendations with business cases

## Key Deliverables

- Patient journey maps with bottleneck identification
- Wait time analysis report with root causes
- Minimum 15 improvement opportunity briefs
- Best practice playbooks (5+ practices documented)
- Resource optimization recommendations
- Process improvement dashboard
- Implementation roadmaps with effort estimates
