# Project Objectives & Success Criteria

**Project Name**: MOH Healthcare System Analysis & Public Health Intelligence  
**Last Updated**: 2 February 2026  
**Version**: 1.0

---

## Project Outcomes

This project aims to deliver actionable intelligence for healthcare policy makers and business decision makers to optimize Singapore's healthcare system and protect public health. The analysis will enable evidence-based decisions across four critical domains:

### 1. Disease Outbreak Detection & Surveillance
**Objective**: Establish early warning systems to identify potential disease outbreaks and emerging health threats

**Key Deliverables**:
- Real-time surveillance dashboards monitoring disease incidence patterns
- Anomaly detection models flagging unusual disease clusters
- Geographic and temporal outbreak risk mapping
- Alert mechanisms for policy makers when outbreak thresholds are exceeded

**Business Impact**: Enable rapid public health response, prevent disease spread, and protect population health through timely interventions

---

### 2. Healthcare Facility Utilization Analysis
**Objective**: Understand the distribution of patient visitation patterns across polyclinics and healthcare facilities

**Key Deliverables**:
- Comprehensive facility utilization profiles (visits by location, time, demographics)
- Geographic access analysis identifying underserved populations
- Demand forecasting models for capacity planning
- Comparative benchmarking across facilities and regions

**Business Impact**: Optimize resource allocation, reduce geographic inequities, and improve healthcare access for all populations

---

### 3. Policy Need Identification
**Objective**: Identify gaps in healthcare system requiring government policy intervention or regulatory action

**Key Deliverables**:
- Systematic gap analysis across care continuum (prevention, primary care, specialty care)
- Evidence-based policy recommendations with quantified impact assessments
- Priority ranking framework for intervention opportunities
- Cost-benefit analysis for proposed policy interventions

**Business Impact**: Guide strategic policy development, prioritize government investments, and address systemic healthcare challenges

---

### 4. Process Improvement Opportunities
**Objective**: Analyze hospitalization and polyclinic processes to identify efficiency gains and patient experience improvements

**Key Deliverables**:
- Patient journey mapping identifying friction points and delays
- Process bottleneck analysis with root cause identification
- Wait time analysis and service efficiency metrics
- Best practice identification from high-performing facilities

**Business Impact**: Enhance patient experience, reduce operational costs, improve service quality, and increase healthcare system efficiency

---

## Success Metrics

The project's effectiveness will be measured through three core success metrics:

### 1. Bottleneck Identification
**Metric**: Number and severity of operational bottlenecks identified across healthcare system

**Targets**:
- Identify minimum 10 critical bottlenecks in patient flow and service delivery
- Quantify impact of each bottleneck (patient wait times, throughput reduction, cost impact)
- Prioritize bottlenecks by severity score (combining volume, impact, and feasibility of resolution)
- Document root causes with supporting evidence for top 5 bottlenecks

**Measurement Approach**:
- Process mining and patient flow analysis
- Service time distribution analysis
- Capacity utilization assessment
- Comparative performance benchmarking

---

### 2. Government Intervention Points
**Metric**: Number of actionable intervention opportunities identified requiring government action

**Targets**:
- Identify minimum 8 high-impact intervention opportunities across 4 domains:
  - **Policy Interventions**: 3+ gaps requiring new regulations or policy changes
  - **Resource Allocation**: 2+ areas requiring increased investment or reallocation
  - **Program Implementation**: 2+ opportunities for new healthcare programs
  - **System Governance**: 1+ structural governance improvements
- Each intervention must include:
  - Quantified problem statement (affected population, severity, cost)
  - Evidence-based recommendation with expected outcomes
  - Implementation feasibility assessment
  - Success metrics for monitoring intervention effectiveness

**Measurement Approach**:
- Gap analysis against healthcare standards and best practices
- Comparative analysis with regional and international benchmarks
- Stakeholder feedback validation
- Cost-effectiveness analysis

---

### 3. Improvement Opportunities
**Metric**: Number of validated improvement opportunities with quantified business value

**Targets**:
- Document minimum 15 improvement opportunities across:
  - **Process Efficiency**: 5+ opportunities to reduce wait times, streamline workflows
  - **Resource Optimization**: 3+ opportunities for better capacity utilization
  - **Quality Enhancement**: 4+ opportunities to improve care quality and patient outcomes
  - **Patient Experience**: 3+ opportunities to enhance patient satisfaction and accessibility
- Each opportunity must include:
  - Current state baseline metrics
  - Expected improvement (quantified outcome)
  - Implementation complexity (low/medium/high)
  - Expected ROI or cost savings
  - Implementation roadmap

**Measurement Approach**:
- Benchmarking analysis (internal and external)
- Statistical analysis of performance variations
- Time-series analysis for trend identification
- Predictive modeling for impact estimation

## Key Stakeholders & Expectations

### Primary Stakeholders: Business Decision Makers

**Role**: Healthcare administrators, hospital executives, and operational leaders

**Expectations**:
- **Operational Intelligence**: Clear insights into facility performance, resource utilization, and operational efficiency
- **Data-Driven Planning**: Evidence-based recommendations for capacity planning, staffing, and service expansion
- **Benchmarking**: Comparative performance metrics to identify best practices and improvement opportunities
- **ROI Justification**: Quantified business cases for proposed investments and process changes
- **Dashboard Access**: Real-time interactive dashboards for monitoring key performance indicators

**Deliverables**:
- Executive dashboards with operational KPIs
- Facility performance scorecards
- Resource optimization recommendations
- Process improvement roadmaps with ROI analysis

---

### Primary Stakeholders: Policy Makers

**Role**: Ministry of Health officials, healthcare regulators, and public health leaders

**Expectations**:
- **Population Health Intelligence**: Comprehensive understanding of disease burden, health trends, and public health threats
- **Policy Evidence**: Rigorous analysis supporting policy development and regulatory interventions
- **Equity Assessment**: Identification of healthcare disparities requiring government action
- **Impact Forecasting**: Predictive analysis of policy intervention outcomes and population-level impacts
- **Strategic Guidance**: Long-term healthcare system planning and governance recommendations

**Deliverables**:
- Policy briefs with evidence-based recommendations
- Public health surveillance reports
- Health equity analysis and disparity mapping
- Strategic planning frameworks for healthcare system development

## Project Approach

### Analysis Framework

1. **Data Foundation** (Weeks 1-2)
   - Establish comprehensive data pipeline from Kaggle MOH dataset
   - Implement data quality validation and monitoring
   - Create unified data model for cross-domain analysis

2. **Exploratory Analysis** (Weeks 3-4)
   - Patient population profiling and segmentation
   - Facility utilization pattern discovery
   - Disease burden assessment
   - Temporal trend identification

3. **Deep-Dive Analysis** (Weeks 5-8)
   - Outcome-specific analysis for each of 4 project outcomes
   - Statistical modeling and hypothesis testing
   - Bottleneck and gap analysis
   - Improvement opportunity identification

4. **Insight Synthesis** (Weeks 9-10)
   - Integrate findings across analysis domains
   - Prioritize recommendations by impact and feasibility
   - Develop stakeholder-specific deliverables
   - Create executive dashboards and reports

### Quality Assurance

- **Statistical Rigor**: All findings supported by appropriate statistical tests with documented confidence levels
- **Data Validation**: Automated quality checks and manual validation of critical findings
- **Peer Review**: Technical review by analytics team before stakeholder delivery
- **Stakeholder Validation**: Iterative feedback loops with end users to ensure relevance and actionability
